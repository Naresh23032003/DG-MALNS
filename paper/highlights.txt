Highlights

* Concurrent search agents offer capacity-stratified moves that are selected jointly
* Joint selection beats rollback and lock commit on 54 to 57 of 60 test instances
* Exact barrier wins single epochs, yet a greedy efficiency rule ends slightly better
* An exact solver needs 2.6 to 8.7 times as long to match it with many alternatives
* Improvement-only agents stall on public knapsack benchmarks with tight capacities
