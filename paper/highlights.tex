\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Parallel search workers offer capacity-stratified moves that are resolved jointly
    \item Joint resolution beats rollback and lock commit on 56 to 58 of 60 test instances
    \item Exact barrier wins single epochs, yet greedy efficiency rule ends slightly better
    \item Beats a time-matched exact solver on all 80 instances with 20 or 40 route candidates
    \item Shadow prices and dynamic quotas add no measurable gain to the method
\end{itemize}
